# August 10, 2026

## What We Built

- Lattice Performance is the active Mission 01 build, with Canny and Fider moved to a visible waiting state while upstream maintainer input is pending.
- A public, source-linked Lattice research record captures the product boundary, customer pain signals, and the decisions behind the first implementation slice.
- The first working Lattice vertical slice now supports Firebase Authentication, organization creation, team and reporting-line imports, role-based access, permission simulation, review templates, review cycles, autosaved reviews, submission, and audit inspection.
- Review-cycle transitions are previewable, block incomplete forward moves, and allow safe reversal without deleting submitted work.
- Firestore rules and emulator tests enforce tenant boundaries, directory permissions, reviewer ownership, review-state restrictions, and immutable audit events.

## What We Fixed

- Workspace authorization is enforced server-side for every organization request and mirrored in Firestore rules for direct client access.
- Employee CSV imports validate all rows before writing, prevent privilege escalation through imported role values, and create reporting relationships atomically.
- Review autosave exposes saving, saved, and failure states; submitted reviews become read-only.
- The public catalog, navigation, roadmap, sitemap, and target brief now consistently identify Lattice as active and Fider as waiting.

## Key Decisions

- The first release targets organizations with 25–250 employees and does not expand into payroll, benefits, recruiting, time tracking, surveys, compensation, succession, native mobile, or a broad integration marketplace.
- Firebase Authentication and Firestore remain the operational backend; Next.js route handlers provide the authorization boundary and audit context.
- Permission modeling uses explicit atomic capabilities and includes a simulator so administrators can inspect a role before applying it.
- Audit events are append-only and include the acting user, organization, action, subject, details, and timestamp.
- The current slice proves the smallest complete review workflow before peer nomination, reminders, calibration, analytics, exports, integrations, and evidence-linked AI summaries are layered onto the same data model.

## Breaking Changes

- Mission 01 now refers to Lattice Performance. The existing Canny → Fider material remains available as a waiting background mission.
