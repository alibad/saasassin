# 2026-08-08

## What We Built

- Builders can explore a ranked catalog of ten high-value SaaS displacement targets and understand the evidence, opportunity, risks, and recommended attack for each.
- Maintainers and adopters can follow a public sequence from the Canny-to-Fider proving mission through Motion and Dovetail replacements.
- Each target has a joinable mission brief with measurable milestones rather than a generic feature wishlist.
- The catalog and roadmap can run from Firestore, while a checked-in research snapshot keeps the site useful before cloud credentials are connected.
- Builder and design-partner interest can be validated and stored through a server-only Firebase route.
- The product now has a distinctive visual identity, responsive interface, social preview, and search-friendly metadata foundation.
- The live Firebase project and protected Firestore database are provisioned, with locked-down client rules and a least-privilege runtime identity.

## What We Fixed

- The initial idea is no longer an unstructured directory concept; it has an operating model based on build, assemble, and back strategies.
- Open-source categories with credible maintainers are protected from unnecessary greenfield fragmentation.
- Firebase failures are explicit: catalog reads fall back safely, while interest submissions never pretend to succeed when persistence is unavailable.

## Key Decisions

- Firestore is the system of record for targets, roadmap phases, and mission interest.
- Direct browser access to Firestore is denied until authentication and role rules are designed.
- Runtime credentials live outside the repository and are referenced through application-default credentials rather than copied into source files.
- The first mission strengthens Fider against Canny before SaaSassin attempts a greenfield product.
- Displacement is measured through migrations and retained usage, not repository creation or visual similarity.
- The custom domain will not become the canonical URL until it resolves over HTTPS.

## Breaking Changes

- None. This is the initial product build.
