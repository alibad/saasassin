# October 26, 2026

## What We Built

- **All ten SaaSassin products now work end to end**, each on its own subdomain with
  its own sign-in, workspaces, roles, audit log and versioned JSON export:
  - **Performance** (Lattice): peer/upward assignments and nominations, deadlines and
    in-app reminders, calibration, review packets with anonymity thresholds and
    acknowledgement, goals, feedback, one-to-ones, employee timeline, reports with
    saved views, scheduled cycle phases, read-only API keys and signed webhooks.
    Imported employees can now sign in as their own directory record.
  - **Research** (Dovetail): interviews from VTT/SRT/text with consent and retention,
    transcript correction, highlights and tags, insights that cite exact evidence
    spans, search, redaction, legal holds, retention runs, restricted folders,
    reports, insight version history and Dovetail import.
  - **Planner** (Motion): tasks with estimates, deadlines, dependencies and
    priorities, a deterministic auto-scheduler that explains every block, locks,
    what-if previews, plan versions with restore, team capacity and ICS export.
  - **Creator** (Kajabi): schools, courses, publishing, public course and catalog
    pages, free enrollment, progress, server-scored quizzes, drip, certificates,
    lesson discussions, analytics, bulk enroll and Kajabi content import.
  - **Feedback** (Canny/Fider): public boards, one vote per person, comments,
    statuses, duplicate merge and unmerge, owners, segments, initiatives, roadmap,
    changelog, Canny import, Fider-shaped export and signed webhooks.
  - **Studies** (Maze): seven block types, skip logic, screeners, anonymous
    participant links, frozen published versions, results, funnels, card-sort
    agreement, CSV/JSON exports and shared read-only results.
  - **Revenue** (Gong): accounts, deals and stage history, call transcripts that
    require recording consent, talk-ratio/tracker/next-step analysis, risk signals,
    scorecards, redaction, coaching clips and assignments, retention.
  - **Community** (Circle): open and private spaces, invite links, posts, threads,
    reactions, mentions, events with RSVP and check-in, moderation with reports and
    appeals, plans and entitlements, and courses.
  - **Mail** (Superhuman): .eml/.mbox import, threading, keyboard-first triage,
    splits, snooze, follow-ups, drafts, snippets, search and a command palette.
  - **Trust** (Vanta): SOC 2 and ISO 27001 control libraries in our own words,
    evidence with expiry, policies with versions and acknowledgements, risk register,
    vendors, readiness dashboard, auditor access with expiry, findings, access
    reviews and a public trust center that shows only what admins publish.
- A shared fleet platform gives every product the same trusted spine: product-only
  sessions, tenant creation and membership checks, append-only audit events and the
  shared export manifest.

## What We Fixed

- The August 10–11 Performance auth and fleet foundation work, which was deployed but
  never committed, is now in git history.
- Product subdomains now route their subpaths (sign-in, public pages) into each app,
  while research briefs stay reachable from product hosts.
- Optional form fields no longer fail Firestore writes (`ignoreUndefinedProperties`).
- Browsers can no longer reach any fleet product data directly; a rules test proves it.

## Key Decisions

- The owner lifted every evidence and upstream gate for this build. Gates are now the
  proof each product owes before customer launch (`docs/fleet/architecture.md` §9),
  not preconditions for building.
- Fleet product data is server-only: Firestore rules deny `products/**` and every
  route re-checks session, membership and role with the Admin SDK.
- Products built natively instead of assembling upstream components; Feedback keeps
  Fider-compatible data so the upstream path stays open.
- Anything needing new credentials (Gmail/Microsoft OAuth, payments, CRM, Vexa,
  email sending, file storage) shows an honest "not configured" state.
- Each product proves its golden workflow against the Firestore emulator.

## Breaking Changes

- Performance employees imported before this change need one CSV re-import so they
  can link their account on sign-in.
- Email/password Performance users must verify their email before claiming an
  imported record.
