# SaaSassin delivery roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation, days 0–14

- Publish the catalog, scoring model, and mission detail pages.
- Connect Firestore and seed the researched portfolio.
- Recruit the first maintainers, builders, and design partners.
- Define clean-room, licensing, governance, and verification standards.

Exit condition: ten qualified builder submissions and one committed design partner.

## Phase 1 — Canny to Fider, days 15–45

- Work with Fider rather than create a competing repository.
- Deliver Canny import, roadmap and changelog workflows, SSO, deployment polish, and regression coverage.
- Operate a hosted reference instance and document migration.

Exit condition: three real migrations and one paying hosted customer.

## Phase 2 — Open Planner, days 46–105

- Deliver tasks, time blocking, Google and Microsoft calendar sync, constraint-based rescheduling, and bring-your-own AI.
- Test with a small cohort for four consecutive weeks.

Exit condition: 20 weekly active users and four-week cohort retention above 40 percent.

## Phase 3 — Open Research Repository, days 76–165

- Deliver document and audio ingestion, transcript correction, tags, clips, semantic search, granular access, self-hosting, and lossless export.
- Complete privacy and security review with design partners.

Exit condition: two organizations migrate a live research archive.

## Phase 4 — Platform, months 6–12

- Add mission proposals, maintainer workspaces, public milestones, verification, funding, and project-health signals.
- Expand the catalog only when each new target has a credible attack strategy and adopter evidence.
