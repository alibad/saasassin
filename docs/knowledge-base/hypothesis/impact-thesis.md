# SaaSassin impact thesis

## Problem

Businesses repeatedly pay high recurring prices for software whose core workflows are understandable, portable, and increasingly buildable by small AI-native teams. Open-source alternatives often fail to displace incumbents not because the code is impossible, but because discovery, product scope, migration, quality assurance, and sustained distribution are fragmented.

## Primary audience

- AI-native builders looking for consequential, bounded products to ship.
- Open-source maintainers who need funding, contributors, product direction, and distribution.
- SaaS customers seeking lower cost, data ownership, self-hosting, and credible exit paths.

## Promise

**Choose a target. Join a mission. Ship the open replacement.**

SaaSassin turns SaaS displacement into a coordinated product program: evidence-backed targets, clean-room requirements, public milestones, verified migrations, and maintained open-source outcomes.

## Core hypothesis

If SaaSassin gives builders a ranked catalog of tractable targets, backs existing maintainers where possible, and measures success through real migrations rather than feature checklists, then small AI-native teams can create durable open-source substitutes faster and with less duplication.

## First-week success

- 100 qualified catalog visitors.
- 10 builder-interest submissions.
- 3 conversations with existing maintainers or potential design partners.
- At least 1 design partner for the first Canny-to-Fider challenge.

## Long-term success

- Verified migrations away from targeted SaaS products.
- Active maintainers and contributors per mission.
- Retained weekly usage of the replacement products.
- Sustainable hosted-support, migration, or enterprise-services revenue.
- A growing share of missions that strengthen existing open-source projects instead of fragmenting them.

## Non-goals

- Copying proprietary code, assets, wording, or trade dress.
- Declaring victory based only on repository creation or feature parity.
- Rebuilding products whose primary moat is a regulated financial rail, proprietary dataset, hardware network, or two-sided marketplace.
- Operating a generic open-source directory with no migration or delivery accountability.
