# Competitive landscape

SaaSassin is not competing with individual open-source SaaS replacements. It should make them more successful.

## Adjacent models

- Open-source alternative directories help with discovery but rarely coordinate delivery or migration.
- Bounty platforms fund issues but usually lack a product thesis, design partners, and adoption metrics.
- Incubators support companies but do not organize work around replacing a named incumbent.
- AI app builders accelerate implementation but do not choose targets, govern projects, or maintain outcomes.

## Strategic differentiation

SaaSassin combines target research, an explicit attack strategy, clean-room scope, milestone funding, design partners, verification, and distribution. Every catalog entry must state whether the right move is to build, assemble existing primitives, or back an existing maintainer.

## Initial target portfolio

The researched portfolio is Dovetail, Lattice, Motion, Kajabi, Canny, Maze, Gong, Circle, Superhuman Mail, and Vanta. The public scoring model weighs economic pain, demand, MVP tractability, open-source whitespace, data portability, and sustainable operating economics.
